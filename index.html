Technique

Movement break

Anxiety is energy with nowhere to go. A short burst of movement gives it somewhere to go.

  • 🌀 Shake out your hands and arms for 15 seconds, like flicking off water
  • 🚶 Walk to the farthest point you can reach in 30 seconds, then walk back
  • 🙆 Reach both arms overhead, stretch tall, exhale as you drop them
  • 🦶 Press your feet hard into the floor for 5 seconds, then release

A note on what this is. Steady is a self-help companion for everyday anxious moments, built from a symptoms-and-solutions worksheet plus widely used, evidence-informed coping techniques. It isn't a diagnosis, treatment, or substitute for care from a therapist or doctor. Your entries sync to your account, so you can pick up where you left off on another device — a local copy is also kept in this browser as a backup. If anxiety is a regular, heavy part of your life, a licensed therapist can help far more than any app — and if you're ever in crisis, please reach out to a crisis line or emergency services in your area right away.
